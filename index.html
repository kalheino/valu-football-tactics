🧤
Goalkeeper backpass rule
If a teammate deliberately passes back to the GK with her foot, the GK cannot pick it up — she must use her feet. Picking it up gives the opposition an indirect free kick.
🔄 Restarting Play — the Basics
These are the ways play starts again after the ball stops or goes out. Handy if you've never played before!
⚽
Kick-off
Starts each half and restarts play after a goal. Taken from the centre spot — the other team must stay outside the centre circle until the ball is kicked.
🙌
Throw-in
When the ball crosses the side line, the team that did NOT touch it last throws it back in. Use both hands, bring the ball from behind your head, and keep both feet on the ground.
🦵
Goal kick
When the attacking team sends the ball out over the goal line (no goal scored), the defending team restarts with a kick from inside the goal area.
🚩
Corner kick
When the defending team sends the ball out over their OWN goal line (no goal), the attacking team gets a kick from the nearest corner of the pitch.
🎯
Free kick
Given to a team after the other team commits a foul. Restart with a kick from where the foul happened — opponents must stand at least about 9 m (roughly 6 big steps) away from the ball.

Teams
11 players per side including the goalkeeper
⏱️
Duration
2 × 20 minutes with a half-time break
🚩
Offside Rule
A player is offside if they are nearer to the opponent's goal line than both the ball and the second-last opponent at the moment the ball is played to them — and they are in the opponent's half. Being level with the last defender is onside. It is only an offence if the player then becomes involved in active play. You cannot be offside directly from a throw-in, corner kick, or goal kick.

⚽
Tips on how to play
🦶
1. Stay on your toes
Keep your weight on the balls of your feet, not your heels. This lets you move in any direction instantly.
🏃‍♀️
2. Go to the ball
When a pass is coming, move towards it. Don't stand still and wait for it to arrive, or a defender will intercept it first.
🎯
3. First touch matters
When the ball arrives, control it in the direction you want to go. A good first touch buys you time and space.
👀
4. Look up before you receive
Scan the pitch before the ball arrives so you already know what you'll do with it. Also look where you want to pass before you kick — glancing at your target early means defenders can't read your next move.
🛡️
5. Body between ball and defender
When shielding the ball, put your body in the way and use it as a shield. Use your arms (not hands) for balance and stay strong and low.
🦵
6. Bend your knees when defending
Stay low and balanced so you can change direction quickly. Standing straight makes you slow.
💨
7. Follow your shot
After you shoot, keep running towards the goal. The rebound often comes back.
🦶
8. When passing or shooting — plant your standing foot right
Place your standing foot next to the ball. Not in front, not behind. This gives you balance and accuracy when you strike.
